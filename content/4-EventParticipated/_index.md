&emsp;During the FCAJ internship, I participated in a total of 3 events, each offering a completely new and memorable experience. Through each event, I gained new perspectives on building projects, learned and acquired knowledge from peers and mentors in the community, ranging from technical domains to soft skills. From there, I learned more about what employers—and more broadly, enterprises—expect in the near future.    

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Saturday Meeting

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00, June 13, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in Event:** Attendee 

&emsp;**Main Content and Key Activities:**
- Listened to in-depth sharing regarding the true role of a DevOps Engineer, clarifying system thinking, operational automation, and methods for applying AI as an assistant to boost work efficiency.
- Observed presentations and practical Side Project demos: Building a scalable URL shortener service on AWS, sharing deployment experiences in Serverless, IaC, CI/CD, and methods for resolving latency and cost challenges.
- Learned real-world data analysis skills (Critical Thinking, Data Storytelling) along with corporate working culture in Multinational Corporations (MNCs), such as No-Blame Post-Mortem culture and global governance standards.
- Accessed Cloud AI career development roadmaps, opportunities to diversify technical roles (Solutions Architect, DevOps, Platform Engineer), and directions for engaging in reputable tech communities.
- Participated in discussions and networking with the community of students and tech engineers in HCMC.

&emsp;**Results and Values Achieved:**
- Properly shaped the roles of DevOps Engineers and Data Analysts, clearly realizing the value of core foundational knowledge (Linux, Networking, Programming) combined with soft skills (Data Storytelling, communication) in MNC environments.
- Mastered hands-on learning methods (Learning by doing) and the process of designing and deploying a real serverless product on AWS from the URL shortener demo project.
- Learned root-cause analytical thinking, personal branding methods on LinkedIn, and expanded connection networks with industry experts in technology.

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00, July 25, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** 36th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, No. 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in Event:** Attendee 

&emsp;**Main Content and Key Activities:**
- Listened to keynote address and industry direction sharing from Mr. Joseph Marazota (Head of Technology, AWS ASEAN) on the shift toward continuous application development cycles and the importance of Human-in-the-loop interactive AI.
- Followed product presentations and live demos from winning teams at the AABW Hackathon:
- One Team (First Place): Multi-channel conversational AI Agent (Zalo/WhatsApp) supporting KFC food ordering, optimizing session memory, and cutting infrastructure costs by 60%.  
- Signal Scout (Second Place): Multi-Agent tool analyzing competitor business strategies and financial reports.  
- Plan V (Third Place): Real-time AI camera system (YOLO v2.6 model) detecting and forecasting crowd congestion in high-density areas.  
- 3KA: Native AI assistant helping Cloud Solution Architects automate architecture design, cost estimation, and IaC code generation (Terraform/CloudFormation).  
- Six Pillar Team: AI engine optimizing Anti-Money Laundering (AML) investigation workflows, reducing false positives and shortening case handling time from days to minutes. Participated in discussions on practical lessons in hackathon project management: clearly defining core problems, building feasible MVPs, managing team conflicts, and working under time pressure.  

&emsp;**Results and Values Achieved:**
- Grasped modern AI application design mindsets (Multi-Agent systems, Human-in-the-loop, AI-native architectures) and how to leverage AWS services to build production-ready applications.
- Understood that over 70% of an AI product's success depends on solving the right business problem rather than technological complexity alone.
- Learned teamwork skills under high pressure, lean mindsets (MVP), and expanded networking with top experts from AWS and the AI tech community in HCMC.
