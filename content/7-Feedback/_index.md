### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
FCAJ community members are open and friendly. The learning and working environment is serious and professional, creating conditions for participants to learn corporate culture knowledge.  

**2. Mentor / Team Admin Support**  
The Team Admin provided enthusiastic, thoughtful, and friendly guidance for those facing difficulties when not understanding an issue. Senior mentors also assisted with procedures, documents, and created conditions for smooth working.

**3. Alignment between work and major**  
Assigned work aligned well with knowledge learned at university while introducing new areas I had not been exposed to before. Thanks to this, I both consolidated foundational knowledge and learned practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship offered great opportunities to train soft skills such as teamwork, communication, time management, and problem solving. Simultaneously, participating directly in real work helped me quickly enhance professional working mindsets.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**  
The working culture at FCAJ respects connectivity and sharing. Team spirit was evident as everyone was always ready to support, listen, and contribute constructive feedback to achieve common goals together.

**6. Policies / Benefits for Interns**  
The company provided very flexible time arrangements, helping interns easily balance university studies and internship work.

### Additional Questions
- What were you **most satisfied** with during your internship?  
  * What I was most satisfied with was the enthusiasm and openness of the Team Admin and senior mentors. The friendly environment helped eliminate initial unfamiliarity, giving me confidence to learn and contribute ideas.
- What do you think the company **needs to improve** for future interns?  
  * For office sessions, senior mentors should provide lectures so interns stay more engaged instead of just sitting and self-studying there.
- If recommending to friends, would you **advise them to intern here**? Why?  
  * The FCAJ environment is very flexible, corporate culture is positive, and everyone always creates the best conditions for young people to gain experience, learn, and grow personally. Therefore, I will recommend it to my friends.

### Suggestions & Wishes
- Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience? 
  * Organize additional periodic 1:1 or group Check-in/Sync-up sessions between Mentors and Interns to timely resolve difficulties and guide learning roadmaps.
  * Develop clear orientation document lists/checklists (Onboarding guide) from the first week so interns can easily grasp overall internship roadmaps. 
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?
  * Next semester I will be a final year student and need to dedicate significant time to my graduation thesis, so I will be unable to continue participating.
